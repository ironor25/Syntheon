# MoneyMan AI

MoneyMan AI is your personal financial assistant designed to help you achieve your financial goals, even if you have no prior financial knowledge. Our AI-driven platform provides personalized advice, budgeting tools, and investment strategies to ensure you stay on track with your financial objectives.

## Features

- **Personalized Financial Advice**: Get tailored recommendations based on your financial situation and goals.
- **Budgeting Tools**: Easily create and manage your budget to keep your spending in check.
- **Investment Strategies**: Receive investment suggestions that align with your risk tolerance and financial targets.
- **Goal Tracking**: Set financial goals and track your progress in real-time.
- **Educational Resources**: Access a wealth of information to improve your financial literacy.

## Getting Started

1. **Sign Up**: Create an account on our platform.
2. **Set Your Goals**: Define your financial goals and timelines.
3. **Connect Your Accounts**: Link your bank and investment accounts for a comprehensive view.
4. **Follow Recommendations**: Implement the personalized advice provided by MoneyMan AI.
5. **Track Progress**: Monitor your progress and adjust your plan as needed.
